Refund & Cancellation Policy

When we refund an order for A Steadier Way Out of Debt, and how to ask.

Last updated 27 October 2026

The guide is a digital PDF that you can open as soon as you pay, and it cannot be returned. So we do not refund for a change of mind, but we will always fix a real problem with your order.

We will refund or replace if

  • You were charged twice for the same order.
  • You paid but never received the guide, and we could not fix it within 48 hours of your message.
  • The file is damaged or will not open, and a working copy does not solve it.
  • You received the wrong file.

How to ask

Email admin.forwardliving@gmail.com within 7 days of buying. Include:

  • your name and the email you used at checkout,
  • your Instamojo payment ID (it is in your payment confirmation email),
  • a short description of what went wrong.

We usually reply within 1 to 2 working days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank) within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

Cancellations

An order cannot be cancelled once payment is complete and the download link has been issued. If your payment failed or is stuck, do not pay again. Email us with the payment ID and we will check it.
