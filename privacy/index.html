Privacy Policy

How Forward Living handles your personal information when you buy A Steadier Way Out of Debt.

Last updated 27 October 2026

Who we are

Forward Living (forwardliving.in) is run by Abhishek Yadav. We sell one product: the digital PDF guide A Steadier Way Out of Debt. In this policy, “we” means Forward Living.

What we collect

  • Checkout details: your name, email address and, if you enter it, phone number. These are collected at checkout so we can deliver the guide and reach you about your order.
  • Payment status: our payment partner, Razorpay, processes your payment. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive only the payment ID, amount, status and the contact details you entered.
  • Messages: anything you send to admin.forwardliving@gmail.com.
  • Basic technical data: your IP address and browser details, which our hosting provider (Vercel) logs to keep the site running and secure.

Why we use it

  • To confirm your payment and send you the PDF.
  • To answer support requests and handle refunds.
  • To keep tax and accounting records.
  • To stop fraud and misuse, such as sharing download links.

We email you about new Forward Living guides only if you have clearly agreed to it. You can opt out at any time.

Who sees your data

We do not sell or rent your data. It is shared only with the services needed to run the store: Razorpay (payments and checkout), Vercel (website hosting), and the email and file-hosting tools that deliver your PDF. Some of these providers store data on servers outside India. We also disclose data when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting rules require. Support emails are kept while they are useful for resolving your request. After that, we delete or anonymise them.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies needed to run it and to complete checkout. If we add analytics later, we will update this page first.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, or make a complaint. Email admin.forwardliving@gmail.com and we will reply within a reasonable time. Some records, like invoices, we must keep by law.

Children

The guide is written for adults dealing with money and debt. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Grievance contact

Abhishek Yadav, Forward Living
admin.forwardliving@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
